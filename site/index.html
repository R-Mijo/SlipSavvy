Why does a parlay get harder to win?

All the selections usually need to win. If two independent bets each win 50% of the time, they both win only 25% of the time. Six independent 60% bets all win about 4.7% of the time.

American odds are prices. Convert each to decimal odds and multiply the prices to calculate a simple parlay payout. Your sportsbook may quote something different. Never add American odds together.
