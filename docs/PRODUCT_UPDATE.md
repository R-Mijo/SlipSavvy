# SlipSavvy: product update — November 6, 2026

## Latest usability update

The retail flow now asks for a bet, odds and stake. Optional price-implied scenarios do the math without asking beginners to invent forecasts. The website has a small working payout preview; the full Python app runs locally. See [quantitative comparison](QUANTITATIVE_METHODS.md).

## What this means to a customer

SlipSavvy is a ticket-research and math assistant. It explains each leg, compares ticket probabilities and payouts, and runs transparent scenarios. For example, it shows why six independent 60% legs have only a 4.6656% chance of all winning. Its current probabilities are supplied by the user or scenario author, not produced by a validated sports prediction model.

## Boundaries

Existing ownership and licensing terms remain in effect. Marketing must describe observed features without promising security, accuracy, compliance, winning bets or production readiness that has not been independently demonstrated.

## Next useful step

The working Python assistant now supports 100,000 simulations, scenario scripts, per-leg evidence notes, weather retrieval and an interactive education tab. The native mobile scaffold is incomplete and is not represented as a finished app.

Before native release: complete screenshot OCR with mandatory review; connect licensed odds, injury and weather sources with timestamps; backtest and calibrate any predictive model on held-out games; handle same-game dependence and settlement rules; test physical iPhone/Android builds; establish store accounts and privacy/support pages; configure subscriptions and cancellation only after terms and pricing are settled.

Commercial positioning: an informed ticket companion, with transparent uncertainty. Show wins per 100 hypothetical tickets alongside the payout. Never call a high simulated payout a high-confidence opportunity. A 49¢/month price is a proposal; annual billing is one option to investigate, not an active subscription.
