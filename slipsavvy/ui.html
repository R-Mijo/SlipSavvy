The odds set the payout. They also tell you the win rate needed to break even at that price. That rate is not a prediction.

Two +100 bets combine to +300 under simple multiplied pricing—not +200. Each is 2× your stake; together they are 4×.

A $5 bet paying $500 total sounds exciting. That is a 100× price, needing a 1% win rate to break even. The payout alone cannot tell you the real win chance.
