What happens when you add legs?

Odds describe a price. A win probability describes an estimate. They are related, but they are not interchangeable.

This demonstration assumes independent legs with the same probability. Real tickets can have different probabilities and correlations.

American odds → break-even probability

Multiply decimal prices when combining legs; do not add American odds or percentages. Two +100 legs give a hypothetical decimal price of 2 × 2 = 4, equivalent to +300, with a 25% break-even chance.

AmericanDecimalBreak-even chance

A $5 ticket returning $500 total has a 100× price and a 1% break-even chance. The advertised payout alone does not tell you the real chance of winning.
